Ongoing advisory

Experienced input as the work develops

For teams that need regular help with product priorities, architecture choices, and delivery trade-offs. We agree a monthly level of support, with scheduled sessions and a defined allowance for reviews and questions.

Process

From enquiry to agreed scope

  1. Send some context

    Tell me what you are working on, the decision you need to make, and any deadline. A link to the product or a short description is enough to start.

  2. Discuss fit and scope

    A short conversation to check I am the right person for this, and to agree what the work covers.

  3. Receive a written scope and fee

    You get the deliverables, the timetable, and the fee in writing before anything begins.

  4. Begin once agreed

    Work starts when the scope and fee are settled, not before.

FAQ

What kind of consulting do you take on?

Mostly AI product reviews, product and technical advisory for small teams, and civic-tech or digital public infrastructure work.

How much does a review cost?

Reviews are quoted as a fixed fee once we have agreed the scope. That ties the price to the work rather than to how long it takes, and it means there are no surprises afterwards.

Do you offer standalone sessions?

Yes. If you need one focused session rather than a full review or ongoing support, I charge $300 USD per hour as a standalone rate.

Do you only advise, or do you build too?

Mostly advise. Some engagements benefit from hands-on technical input or product direction, and that can be scoped separately where it makes sense.

Do you work remotely?

Yes. I am based in Barbados and work remotely with teams in other places.
